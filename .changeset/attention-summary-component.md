Add `AttentionSummary`, the patient portal's home summary of what needs the patient's attention: next visit, results to review, unread messages, balance due and refills in progress, each with a button to act on it. Covers loading, nothing-due and partly-unavailable states.

StatCard gets an opt-in `tone="attention"` for numbers the person needs to act on; routine metrics keep the default look.
